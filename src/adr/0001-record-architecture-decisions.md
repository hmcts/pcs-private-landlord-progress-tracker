## Context

The Private Landlord workstream will make technical decisions that people need to understand
later: stakeholders, new team members and other HMCTS teams.

## Decision

We will record significant decisions as architecture decision records (ADRs) in this repository,
in the [MADR](https://adr.github.io/madr/) format, and publish them on this site.

## Consequences

- Each decision has a short, permanent record of its context and the options considered.
- Changing a decision means writing a new record that supersedes the old one.
- This site is public, so records must not include anything that should not be published.
