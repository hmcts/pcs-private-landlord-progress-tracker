QA Summary

Unit test coverage (96.0%/95.5%) and SonarCloud Quality Gates have passed for pcs-api and pcs-frontend at their latest master commits (227c6c6, 8e79648, 25 Nov 2026). Integration, contract, smoke and functional tests passed on master for both repos, and the release candidate deployed successfully to AAT.

However, the Warrant of Restitution and general-application enforcement journeys this epic covers have their automated end-to-end tests skipped in pcs-api (enforcement.warrantOfRestitution.spec.ts in full, and enforcement.spec.ts:539), and no citizen-facing (CUI) design has yet been found for the claimant's enforcement journey, so pcs-frontend has no dedicated end-to-end coverage for this feature either. None of the 27 stories and tasks under HDPI-10221 are done or carry Product Owner sign-off, and none carry a Test Complete comment, so no manual regression testing is recorded.

QA Recommendation
